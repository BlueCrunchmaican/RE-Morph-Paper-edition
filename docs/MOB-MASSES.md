# Mob masses (Survival Mode)

Generated by `tools/gen_mob_masses.py` from Minecraft 26.3's mob sizes and the default `survival.yml`.

A creature's mass is iChun's formula: width × width × height × 1000 kg. It decides three things in Survival Mode:

- **Whether you can absorb it:** your Absorbable Mass upgrade must reach its mass (the sections below).
- **The biomass a kill gives:** mass × 0.3 × your Biomass Efficiency (10% at the start, 55% fully upgraded), never more than your storage has room for.
- **What morphing into it costs:** mass × 0.01, rounded up, at least 1.

Babies are about an eighth of the adult's mass (half the size each way), so they give and cost less. A player is 648 kg. Servers can change any mob's mass in `survival.yml` (`biomass.multipliers`, `biomass.masses`).
